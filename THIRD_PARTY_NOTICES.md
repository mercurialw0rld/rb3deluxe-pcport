The RB3 Deluxe host and launcher build on the native compatibility code and WinForms launcher in [Green Day: Rock Band for Windows](https://github.com/mercurialw0rld/greendayrockband-recomp) and the tooling and SDK patches in [The Beatles: Rock Band for PC](https://github.com/mercurialw0rld/thebeatlesrockband-pcport).

ReXGlue and Xenia attribution and the SDL3 license are in `licenses/`. `tools/FIFA-reference-LICENSE.txt` preserves the notice from the FIFA graphics-patch reference. [Rock Band 3 Deluxe](https://rb3dx.milohax.org/) is by MiloHax. Use the project with your own local game files.

The launcher displays the original Rock Band 3 artwork © 2010 Harmonix Music Systems from the [Libretro thumbnails cover archive](https://github.com/libretro-thumbnails/Nintendo_-_Wii/blob/master/Named_Boxarts/Rock%20Band%203%20%28USA%29.png).
