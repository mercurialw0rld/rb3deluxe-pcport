Rock Band 3 Deluxe for Windows v0.1.0-preview.

Download **RockBand3Deluxe-v0.1.0-preview-windows-x64.zip**, extract the complete application, and run **RockBand3DeluxeLauncher.exe**.

Prepare your own extracted Xbox 360 Rock Band 3 files. Download the Xbox 360 **Main Download** or **Main + Custom Characters** edition from the MiloHax website. Merge its `default.xex` and `gen` into your extracted base game, then select that `default.xex` in **Choose XEX**.
